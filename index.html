About Us

Divine Grace Ministries is a vibrant and dynamic Christian community dedicated to spreading the message of God's love and grace. Our mission is to empower individuals to live a life of faith, hope, and purpose through worship, teaching, and service. We believe in the transformative power of God's Word and strive to create an environment where people can grow spiritually, emotionally, and socially.

Our Mission

To reach people with the Gospel,build strong families,and raise believer who wil make a positive impact in their communities.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of hope and light in the world,bringing people closer to God and helping them discover their purpose in life.
